🚀 The Next Competitive Advantage in AI May Not Be Smarter Models — But Self-Improving Agents

Today, I explored the concept of **Self-Improving AI Agents**, and it shifted how I think about the future of AI systems.

Most conversations focus on building more capable models.

But a more interesting question is:

**What happens when agents can learn from their own actions, evaluate outcomes, and improve over time?**

A traditional AI agent can execute a task.

A self-improving agent can:

• Execute a task
• Evaluate the outcome
• Learn from feedback
• Refine its strategy
• Perform better in future iterations

This feels like a significant shift.

As AI moves from generating information to executing workflows, the ability to continuously improve may become a key differentiator for businesses deploying AI at scale.

For founders and operators, this raises an important consideration:

The long-term value may not come from the model itself, but from the feedback loops, evaluation systems, and proprietary workflows that help agents get better over time.
